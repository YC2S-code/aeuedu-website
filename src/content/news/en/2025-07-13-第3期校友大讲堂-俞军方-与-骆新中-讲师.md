## **The 3rd Alumni Master Talk**

**Speakers: Yu Junfang & Luo Xinzhong**

**Smart Transformation & Business Breakthrough: Practical Strategies for the New Business Landscape**

As business competition continues to intensify, entrepreneurs and executives must adapt with vision and strategy. Hosted by the **Asia Euro University Executive Program**, this distinguished lecture brings together renowned speakers to share practical insights and proven approaches for driving business transformation and sustainable growth.

![](/images/uploads/ai.jpg)

**Mr. Yu Junfang**, a distinguished expert in digital transformation and the telecommunications industry with over 30 years of executive leadership experience, will deliver a keynote on **"Smart Transformation for Cambodian Enterprises."**

Drawing on China's successful digital transformation practices, he will share practical strategies to help businesses leverage digital technologies, enhance operational efficiency, and achieve sustainable growth in Cambodia's evolving market.

![](/images/uploads/48.png)

**Professor Luo Xinzhong**, a leading expert in business model innovation and industrial economics, will present **"Breaking Through the New Global Business Landscape."** 

He will share strategic insights into global economic trends and practical approaches that help enterprises strengthen competitiveness, drive innovation, and achieve sustainable growth in an increasingly complex business environment.

![](/images/uploads/49.png)

As digital transformation and the global business landscape continue to evolve, this distinguished lecture offers entrepreneurs and business leaders practical strategies, forward-looking insights, and valuable networking opportunities. Join us to strengthen your leadership, expand your business perspective, and connect with industry experts shaping the future of enterprise.
