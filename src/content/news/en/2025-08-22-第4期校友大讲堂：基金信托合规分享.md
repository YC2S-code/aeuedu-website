### **4th Alumni Lecture Series: Fund Trust & Compliance**

On **23 August 2025**, the **4th Alumni Lecture Series** gathered alumni and finance professionals to explore the latest trends in **fund trusts and regulatory compliance**. Participants visited **BAMC**, Cambodia's first public fund management company, where **CEO Lü Wenzhen**, an Executive Leadership Program alumnus, shared practical insights into fund operations, product design, and compliance, highlighting the launch of Cambodia's first public fund as a milestone in the country's financial market development.

![](/images/uploads/44.png)

BAMC's public fund achieved a **31% return in 13 months** and became **Cambodia's first fund listed on the Bloomberg Terminal**, marking a major milestone for the country's financial market.

**Key Highlights:**

* **For Investors:**

   Entry from 

  **US$200**

  , making investing more accessible.
* **For the Market:**

   Promotes capital market growth and better fund allocation.
* **For Cambodia:**

   Enhances global visibility and attracts international investors.

### **Featured Sharing: Insights & Practical Experience**

**1. Lü Wenzhen**
**Topic:** *The Significance of Cambodia's First Public Fund*

He explained the strategic value of Cambodia's first public fund from **three perspectives**:

* **Investors:**

   Expanding access to investment opportunities.
* **Financial System:**

   Strengthening the capital market.
* **National Economy:**

   Advancing financial development and global integration.

![](/images/uploads/基金3.jpg)

### **2. Hans (CEO of Dibao Trust)**

**Topic:** *The Core Role of Trusts in the Financial System*

Hans shared practical insights into how **trusts** support **fund custody, real estate holdings, and equity management**, while highlighting their value in **cross-border investment** and **risk protection**.

![](/images/uploads/基金4.jpg)

### **3. Zhang Dazhi (CEO, South Asia Business Research Institute)**

**Topic:** *New Trends in Corporate Compliance in Cambodia*

Drawing on his business experience, Zhang Dazhi shared the key compliance challenges facing enterprises in Cambodia:

* **Environmental:**

   Environmental approvals are essential for business operations.
* **Tax:**

   Stronger tax enforcement requires accurate financial reporting.
* **Labour:**

   Foreign employee quotas must comply with legal regulations.

He emphasized that **compliance is not a cost, but a long-term investment for sustainable business growth.**
