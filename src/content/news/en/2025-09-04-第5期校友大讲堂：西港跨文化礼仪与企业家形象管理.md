# 5th Alumni Masterclass: Cross-Cultural Etiquette & Executive Image

At our September 5th, two veteran CEO alumni shared real-world insights on bridging international and Cambodian-Chinese business etiquette—equipping entrepreneurs with practical strategies to elevate their executive presence and expand their global vision.

![](/images/uploads/43.png)

### **Cross-Cultural Business Etiquette: Building Stronger Cambodia–China Partnerships**

**Wang Guangqing**, Division General Manager of Golden Silver Bay Hotel and Executive Leadership Program participant, shared practical insights into **Chinese and Cambodian business etiquette**. 

Drawing on his cross-border management experience, he explained how cultural understanding and professional etiquette help build trust, strengthen business relationships, and create successful long-term partnerships between Chinese and Cambodian enterprises.

![](/images/uploads/西港3.jpg)

### **Image Capital: A Strategic Investment for Entrepreneurs**

**Jing Yi**, Co-founder of Medical Beauty Group and Executive Leadership Program participant, shared how **personal image is a strategic asset for business leaders**. 

He emphasized that confidence, professionalism, and personal branding go beyond appearance, helping entrepreneurs strengthen leadership presence and create greater business value.

![](/images/uploads/西港4.jpg)

### **Leadership Image Capital: A Long-Term Business Asset**

Jing Yi illustrated how **personal image and leadership presence** can strengthen team confidence, build trust with investors and partners, and enhance brand value. He also introduced a **three-step image transformation strategy**, emphasizing that investing in personal branding is a strategic investment that creates lasting competitive advantages for both entrepreneurs and their businesses.

![](/images/uploads/西港5.jpg)

### **Event Impact & Future Outlook**

The interactive discussion encouraged participants to exchange ideas on **cross-cultural communication** and **leadership image management**. Professor Luo Xinzhong concluded that today's entrepreneurs must represent not only their businesses but also their corporate culture, emphasizing that **cultural awareness, professional etiquette, and personal branding** are essential skills for succeeding in the global business environment.

![](/images/uploads/西港6.jpg)

 **Event Impact & Future Outlook**

The successful Alumni Lecture Series reflected the Asia Euro Postgraduate School's commitment to **combining academic learning with real-world business practice**. Through continuous knowledge sharing and alumni collaboration, the platform empowers entrepreneurs to broaden their perspectives, build valuable partnerships, and seize new opportunities. Looking ahead, the School will continue hosting diverse alumni lectures to strengthen the business community and promote Cambodia–China economic cooperation and regional development.
