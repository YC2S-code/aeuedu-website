# 6th Alumni Masterclass: Navigating Tax Compliance & AI

on Friday, October 17th, This session focus on "AI-Driven Transformation & Tax Compliance in Cambodia." We’re bringing in two veteran alumni to share sharp, practical strategies for succeeding in today's local market. This is your exclusive platform to network, share resources, and collaborate with fellow entrepreneurs in a shifting economy. 

![](/images/uploads/友友1.png)

**Lin Hongyu** — Cambodia Investment & Tax: Policy, Risk, and Compliance

**Lin Hongyu**, General Manager of Huaqi CPA Firm and an **8th CEO Class alumna**, will unpack Cambodia's current tax and investment landscape. She breaks down the latest policies and tax incentives to give you a clear view of the local regulatory environment.

More importantly, she highlights common tax traps and shares real-world case studies, equipping your business with practical, compliant strategies to operate safely and efficiently in Cambodia.

![](/images/uploads/友友2.jpg)

**Long Jihong** — AI-Driven Transformation: The Next Step for Cambodian Businesses

**Long Jihong**, GM of Kingdee Software's Cambodia Office, GM of Yake Technology's Software Division, and a **1st CEO Class alumnus**, explores how local enterprises can leverage AI to upgrade and scale.

Tailored to the Cambodian market, he breaks down practical AI applications that boost operational efficiency and market competitiveness. He offers business leaders a clear, actionable roadmap to overcome current challenges and drive a successful digital transformation.

![](/images/uploads/友友3.jpg)

### **Deepening Connections & Driving Growth**

Through the expertise of **Long Jihong** and **Lin Hongyu**, you'll walk away with a clear roadmap for AI-driven transformation and a solid grip on local tax compliance.

Whether you're looking to scale your current operations with smart tech or structure a new investment in Cambodia, this session delivers the exact insights you need to grow steadily, operate safely, and stay ahead of the curve.
