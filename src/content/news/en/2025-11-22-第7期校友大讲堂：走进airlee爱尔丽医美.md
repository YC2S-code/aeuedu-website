### **7th Alumni Lecture Series: A Visit to Airlee Aesthetic Medicine**

On **23 November 2025**, alumni gathered for an exclusive visit to **Airlee Aesthetic Medicine**, gaining firsthand insights into the medical aesthetics industry while exchanging ideas, strengthening connections, and exploring new opportunities for business growth and collaboration.

![](/images/uploads/爱尔丽1.jpg)

### **Guest Speaker: Director Mimi**

**Founder of Airlee Aesthetic Medicine, Cambodia | Executive Leadership Program Batch 8**

Director Mimi shared insights on **Cambodia's medical aesthetics and regenerative medicine industry**, covering market trends, business operations, and entrepreneurial experiences. Participants also toured the **Airlee Aesthetic Medicine Center**, gaining a deeper understanding of the industry's innovation and growth potential.

![](/images/uploads/爱尔丽2.jpg)

### **Key Highlights**

* Cambodia's medical aesthetics market and industry trends.
* Latest developments in regenerative and functional medicine.
* Best practices in clinic operations, safety, and customer experience.
* Real entrepreneurial insights for building a business in Cambodia.
* Industry evolution from aesthetic treatments to holistic health management.

Participants also toured the **Airlee Aesthetic Medicine Center**, gaining a deeper understanding of Cambodia's growing medical aesthetics and wellness industry.

### **Guest Speaker: Tan Ling**

**CEO, Extraordinary Business Consulting | Executive Leadership Program Batch 8**

Tan Ling shared practical guidance on **Cambodia–China marriage registration procedures**, explaining key policies, legal requirements, and the application process based on her years of professional experience.

![](/images/uploads/爱尔丽3.jpg)
