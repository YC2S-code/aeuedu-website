# 8th Alumni Lecture Series: Real Estate Legal Risk Management & Practical Insights

This session features two distinguished alumni entrepreneurs sharing valuable insights on **real estate legal risk management, digital transformation, and Cambodia–China cross-border business opportunities**.

The event aims to strengthen alumni connections, encourage knowledge exchange, and foster strategic collaboration in the evolving business landscape. It will be held on **16 January 2026 at 3:00 PM** on the **23rd Floor, The Gateway, Phnom Penh**. Alumni and business professionals are warmly invited to attend.

![](/images/uploads/42.png)

### **Li Hongfeng — The Road to Enterprise Digital Transformation in the AI Era**

Li Hongfeng, Executive Vice President of the Cambodia–China Industrial Digital Transformation Promotion Association and a distinguished alumnus of the **7th Executive Leadership Program**, will share practical strategies for driving enterprise digital transformation in the age of AI.

he explores how artificial intelligence is reshaping management, operations, and marketing, while offering actionable insights to help businesses accelerate digital innovation and achieve sustainable growth.

![](/images/uploads/小2.jpg)

### **Liu Yong — Cambodia–China Trade: Bonded Zones & Cross-border E-commerce**

Liu Yong, Partner at Cambodia Central United Law Firm and a distinguished alumnus of the **4th Executive Leadership Program**, will share practical insights into **Cambodia–China trade, bonded zone policies, and cross-border e-commerce**. Drawing on his legal expertise, he will highlight key regulations, compliance requirements, and business strategies for companies expanding into cross-border markets.

![](/images/uploads/小3.jpg)

Participants will gain practical insights into **AI-driven digital transformation**, **Cambodia–China trade**, and **cross-border e-commerce** through the expert sharing of **Li Hongfeng** and **Liu Yong**. The forum offers valuable business strategies, legal guidance, and market insights to help enterprises innovate, expand with confidence, and achieve sustainable growth.
