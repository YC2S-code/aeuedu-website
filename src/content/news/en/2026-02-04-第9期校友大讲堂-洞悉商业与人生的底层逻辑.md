### **Alumni Lecture – Understanding the Fundamentals of Business and Life**

Hosted by the **Asia Euro University Postgraduate School**, the **9th Alumni Lecture Series** Held on **5 February 2026** at **Angkor Art, 6F, Phnom Penh**, the event explores the fundamental principles behind business success and personal growth through the real-world experiences of accomplished entrepreneurs.

The forum features two distinguished alumni from the **9th Executive President Program**—**Mr. Fang** and **Mr. Achong**—who will share their entrepreneurial journeys across the premium wellness industry and digital media. 

Their presentations combine strategic life perspectives with practical digital marketing expertise, offering attendees valuable insights into leadership, innovation, and sustainable business growth.

![](/images/uploads/40.png)

**Mr. Fang**, founder of **DIA Bird’s Nest** and a distinguished alumnus of the **9th Executive President Program**, will present **"Understanding the Script of Life."** Drawing on his entrepreneurial journey and leadership experience, he will share insights into self-awareness, strategic thinking, and aligning personal strengths with business opportunities to achieve sustainable growth.

![](/images/uploads/小小3.jpg)

**Mr. Achong**, founder of **Spark Media** and a fellow alumnus of the **9th Executive President Program**, will deliver **"The Development Trends of Cambodia’s Digital Media."** Focusing on short-form video and personal branding, he will explore the latest digital marketing strategies and demonstrate how individuals and businesses can build influential brands and thrive in the evolving digital landscape.

![](/images/uploads/小小4.jpg)

By combining these two complementary sessions, participants will gain both a strategic mindset and practical digital capabilities. 

Mr. Fang will offer valuable perspectives on leadership, self-awareness, and long-term business vision, while Mr. Achong will provide actionable strategies for digital branding and short-video marketing in today's evolving media landscape. 

Together, their insights will equip attendees to strengthen their leadership, embrace digital transformation, and seize new opportunities for sustainable business growth.
