### **11th Alumni Leadership Forum | Cambodia's New Investment Cycle**

On the afternoon of **April 9, 2026**, the **Asia Euro University Faculty of International Business (AEU FIB)**successfully hosted the **11th Alumni Leadership Forum** at Morgan Tower 19A. 

Bringing together distinguished alumni, the forum explored Cambodia's evolving investment landscape, providing valuable insights into emerging opportunities, key challenges, and the country's new investment cycle.

![](/images/uploads/校友1.jpg)

## 01 Opening Remarks

![](/images/uploads/校友2.jpg)

The event commenced with opening remarks by **Ms. Carey Liu**, Class Coordinator of the **Asia Euro University Faculty of International Business (AEU FIB)**. She emphasized that the Alumni Leadership Forum serves not only as a platform for knowledge sharing but also as a testament to the strength and value of the alumni network. 

She noted that the School is committed to developing leaders with vision, sound judgment, and purpose. The return of two distinguished alumni as guest speakers reflected the lasting impact of the University's education and set an inspiring tone for the forum.

## 02 Sharing by Mr. Chu Zexu

![](/images/uploads/校友3.jpg)

**Mr. Chu Zexu**, CEO of Morgan Group and an alumnus of the **8th AEU FIB cohort**, shared practical insights drawn from his years of business experience in Cambodia. He explained that as the market continues to mature, sustainable success depends not on short-term opportunities but on building long-term competitive advantages through strong local relationships, regulatory compliance, and brand credibility. His presentation encouraged alumni to focus on developing lasting capabilities and strategic vision for long-term success in Cambodia's evolving investment landscape.

![](/images/uploads/校友4.jpg)

## 03 Sharing by Mr. Yang Zongheng

**Mr. Yang Zongheng**, CEO of Meichen Real Estate and an alumnus of the **10th AEU FIB cohort**, shared practical insights into Cambodia's real estate market based on his industry experience. He outlined key investment considerations, including location selection, property evaluation, and developer credibility, offering a practical framework for informed decision-making. 

![](/images/uploads/校友5.jpg)

He emphasized that choosing the right investment is more important than timing the market, and that disciplined investment strategies and clear evaluation standards are essential for long-term success.

The success of the forum was made possible through the enthusiastic participation of alumni and distinguished guests.
