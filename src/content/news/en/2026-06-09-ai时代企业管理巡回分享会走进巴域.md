## AEUEDU Partners with Business Leaders to Navigate AI-Driven Management Transformation and Accelerate High-Quality Growth

On July 10, 2026, AEUEDU successfully hosted the "AI-Era Enterprise Management: Strategic Transformation for Competitive Advantage" Roadshow and International EMBA Information Session in Bavet.

- - -

The event featured Mr. Alex Chen Qiyan, Marketing Faculty of Asia Euro Postgraduate School, who delivered a keynote on "AI-Era Enterprise Management: Strategic Transformation for Competitive Advantage."

![](/images/uploads/巴域-2.png)

**Mr. Chen** emphasized that while AI will not replace entrepreneurs, those who embrace AI will lead the future of business. He noted that long-term success depends not only on technology, but also on strategic vision, leadership, continuous learning, and the ability to innovate.

- - -

**Ms. Liu Kaili**, **Board Member**, introduced the International **EMBA** program, while Ms. Liang Kaixin, Director of Academic Affairs, presented the **ELP** (Entrepreneur Leadership Program), offering business leaders a forward-looking platform for leadership development and lifelong learning.

![](/images/uploads/巴域3.jpg)

She emphasized that the AI era is redefining business competition, where success is driven not only by products and markets, but by strategic thinking, management excellence, global vision, and lifelong learning. 

She described the International EMBA as more than an academic qualification—it is a platform for entrepreneurs to elevate leadership, broaden perspectives, and build valuable international business networks.

![](/images/uploads/1ll-copy.jpeg)

- - -

**Ms. Liang Kaixin, Director of Academic Affairs**, then introduced the ELP (Entrepreneur Leadership Program) to the participating business leaders. She emphasized that the growth of an entrepreneur is a lifelong journey of continuous learning and self-improvement. 

![](/images/uploads/巴域5.png)

She further explained that the Asia Euro Postgraduate School ELP (Entrepreneur Leadership Program) is designed around the practical needs of business leaders, focusing on leadership, strategic decision-making, marketing, organizational management, and business innovation. 

![](/images/uploads/1ll.png)

Guided by its vision of ***"Rooted in Cambodia, Connected to ASEAN, and Oriented to the World,"*** Asia Euro Postgraduate School is committed to building a lifelong learning platform for entrepreneurs. Through its International **EMBA and ELP** (Entrepreneur Leadership Program), the School integrates world-class faculty, industry expertise, business practice, and a strong alumni network to cultivate visionary leaders with strategic thinking, global perspectives, and innovative capabilities.

Looking ahead, **Asia Euro Postgraduate School** will continue expanding its Enterprise Management Roadshow across Phnom Penh, Sihanoukville, Bavet, and other key economic regions of Cambodia.

By strengthening entrepreneur development, university–industry collaboration, and international exchange, the School aims to empower more businesses to embrace the AI era, anticipate global economic trends, accelerate management transformation, and drive sustainable, high-quality growth.
