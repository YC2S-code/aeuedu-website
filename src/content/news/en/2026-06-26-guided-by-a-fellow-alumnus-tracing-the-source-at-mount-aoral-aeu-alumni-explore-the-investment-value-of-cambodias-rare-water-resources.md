Rugged Mountain Roads, Undiminished Enthusiasm Recently, AEU International Business School alumnus and founder of Quantum Philanthropy, Mr. Li Mingyang, personally organized and led a delegation of AEU alumni and members of the Business Entrepreneurs Private Board deep into the Aoral Wildlife Sanctuary in Cambodia. The group first toured the complete production process at a water bottling plant, then trekked upstream to the natural water source at Chrok La Eang Waterfall. Guided by a fellow alumnus and united by AEU camaraderie, this on-the-ground industry exploration bore witness to the investment value and brand potential behind this rare Cambodian water resource.

![](/images/uploads/news/guided-by-a-fellow-alumnus-tracing-the-source-at-mount-aoral-aeu-alumni-explore-the-investment-value-of-cambodias-rare-water-resources/2.jpg)

Mr. Li Mingyang wears many hats: founder of Quantum Philanthropy, and also one of the principal investors behind the Aoral mountain water plant. As a fellow AEU alumnus, he took the initiative to organize this trip, inviting fellow graduates and Private Board members to visit the water source in person—connecting his own industry resources with the AEU alumni network. Driven by the candid intention to "show his fellow alumni the reality, firsthand," he set this on-the-ground industry due-diligence expedition in motion.

![](/images/uploads/news/guided-by-a-fellow-alumnus-tracing-the-source-at-mount-aoral-aeu-alumni-explore-the-investment-value-of-cambodias-rare-water-resources/3.jpg)

### Into the Plant · A Full-Line Production Tour

The first stop of the visit took the group through the plant's entire production line, from raw materials to finished product, under the guidance of professional staff—offering a firsthand look at the quality-control logic and technical investment behind every stage of the process.
