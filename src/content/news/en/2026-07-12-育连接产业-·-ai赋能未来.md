## **Education Meets Industry · AI Shapes the Future**

On **13 July 2026**, the **Asia Euro Postgraduate School** successfully hosted the **Alumni Distinguished Speaker Series & Industry–University Partnership Press Conference** in Phnom Penh. During the event, the School signed a **Strategic Partnership Agreement** with **Unicorn 9×16 MCN**, strengthening collaboration in AI education, industry innovation, and international cooperation to advance the future of industry–academia integration.

![](/images/uploads/签约1.png)

### Connecting Education with Industry in the AI Era

At the event, **Neak Oknha Ly Kunthai**, Director of the Entrepreneurship & Investment Development Center at the **Asia Euro Postgraduate School**, emphasized that AI is transforming global business and redefining the future of higher education.

He noted that business education must go beyond the classroom by connecting academia with industry, technology, and international collaboration.

> **"Tomorrow's leaders must understand both business and AI. Education should empower students to solve real industry challenges through innovation and practical experience."**

He added that this strategic partnership represents a significant step in advancing **AI-Driven Business Education**, integrating AI into academic programs, industry projects, and global collaboration to develop the next generation of digitally empowered business leaders.

- - -

### **AI-Powered Enterprise Management | From Trends to Real-World Practice**

The event also featured the **AI Enterprise Management Forum**, where industry experts explored the practical application of AI in business management, strategic decision-making, digital marketing, and innovation.

Through real-world case studies, entrepreneurs and alumni gained valuable insights into how AI can enhance operational efficiency, optimize management processes, and create new business opportunities—while developing the digital mindset and leadership capabilities essential for success in the AI era.

![](/images/uploads/签约3.jpg)

![](/images/uploads/签约4.jpg)

### **Building an International AI-Powered Industry–Academia Platform**

The event concluded with the signing of a **Strategic Industry–University Partnership Agreement** between the **Asia Euro Postgraduate School** and **Unicorn 9×16 MCN**, marking the beginning of a long-term collaboration to accelerate AI innovation and talent development.

The partnership will focus on:

* **AI-Driven Business Education**
* **AI Talent Development**
* **Enterprise Digital Transformation**
* **Digital Content & MCN Ecosystem**
* **New Media E-commerce & Brand IP Incubation**
* **Executive Education & Industry Practice**
* **China–Cambodia Cross-Border Innovation & International Collaboration**

![](/images/uploads/签约5.jpg)

双方将充分发挥高校教育资源与企业产业优势，共同打造集**教学、实践、创新、创业**于一体的国际化产学合作平台，推动教育与产业深度融合，实现人才培养与产业发展的双向赋能。

## **签约不是终点**

### 而是合作成果落地的开始

本次发布会最大的亮点，不仅是双方正式签署战略合作协议，更同步发布合作后的首个重点项目——

# **《双境追凶》**

## **柬中联合打造AI技术赋能真人竖屏微短剧**

目前，该项目已完成剧本开发，并计划于**2026年8月正式开机拍摄，同月上线发布。**

《双境追凶》将采用**AI技术辅助创作**与**真人实景拍摄**相结合的制作模式，融合数字影视制作、新媒体传播及跨国文化创意资源，探索AI技术与影视创作深度融合的新方向。

![](/images/uploads/签约6.png)

这不仅是双方战略合作后的首项落地成果，也是亚欧大学国际商学院推动AI教育、产业实践与国际合作的重要实践。未来，学院将持续引入更多真实产业项目，让学生走进项目、走进企业，在实践中学习，在创新中成长，真正实现"课堂连接产业、学习创造价值"的人才培养理念。

## **面向未来**

### 共创AI时代商业教育新生态

未来，亚欧大学国际商学院将继续依托创业投资发展中心，深化与国内外优秀企业及产业伙伴的合作，持续推动**AI驱动商业教育（AI-Driven Business Education）**，促进教育、产业与科技深度融合，构建更加开放、多元、国际化的发展生态。

学院将以教育为根基，以产业为平台，以创新为动力，持续培养能够驾驭AI时代、引领产业发展的新时代商业领袖，为柬埔寨及东盟数字经济高质量发展贡献教育力量。
