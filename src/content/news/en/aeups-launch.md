---
title: AEU International Business School (AEUPS) Officially Established
date: 2025-01-15
summary: Built on more than two decades of Asia Euro University's academic foundation, AEUPS is established to focus on EMBA and PEP executive education.
---

Over more than two decades, Asia Euro University grew from a college into a university. In January 2025, AEU International Business School (AEUPS) was officially established, marking a new chapter focused on executive business education.

AEUPS focuses on the EMBA and PEP (Professional Education Programs), serving founders, senior executives, and decision-makers. It is positioned as Cambodia's first Chinese-taught executive business school platform, using RCEP as a gateway to ASEAN markets and networks.
