In this alumni spotlight, we speak with a graduate of our EMBA program about their academic journey and entrepreneurial path.

The alumnus shared that the program's strategic management and leadership modules played a key role in their company's transformation — particularly amid intensifying regional competition, where classroom discussions and peer exchange helped reshape their long-term business direction.

Today, the company they lead has successfully expanded into multiple ASEAN markets, and they remain closely connected with the school, regularly participating in alumni events and industry talks.
