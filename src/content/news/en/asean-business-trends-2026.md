As regional economic integration accelerates, the ASEAN market is showing new characteristics in its business environment. Digital transformation, supply chain realignment, and easier cross-border investment are now top of mind for business leaders.

This article examines three key trends: first, improved digital infrastructure is lowering the barriers to entering new markets; second, regional supply chains are shifting toward greater diversification and localization; and third, closer policy coordination across countries is creating a more favorable environment for cross-border expansion.

For founders and senior executives, understanding these trends — and adjusting strategy accordingly — will be key to staying competitive in the regional market.
