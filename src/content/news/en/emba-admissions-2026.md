AEU International Business School (AEUPS) has announced that admissions for the 2026 Fall EMBA cohort are now open. This cohort will focus on corporate strategy, organizational leadership, and regional market expansion, taught by renowned scholars and industry experts from around the world.

Applicants are expected to have relevant management experience, with priority given to founders, partners, and senior executives.

Eligible candidates are encouraged to submit their applications early, as places are limited and offers will be made based on application order and overall evaluation.
