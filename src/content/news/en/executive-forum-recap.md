The third AEU Executive Forum concluded successfully in Phnom Penh, bringing together entrepreneurs, senior executives, and faculty and students from across the region.

Themed "ASEAN Market Opportunities and Sustainable Business Growth," the forum featured industry leaders sharing practical insights, alongside a roundtable discussion on corporate strategy amid regional economic integration.

The school said it plans to continue hosting similar events, creating a platform for students and alumni to connect, learn, and collaborate.
