AEU International Business School (AEUPS) has signed strategic partnership agreements with several leading regional companies, marking a major step forward in its industry collaboration efforts.

Under the agreements, partner companies will offer EMBA and PEP students company visits, industry mentorship, and practical placement opportunities — helping students apply classroom learning to real business scenarios.

The school says it will continue expanding its partner network, building a broader platform of industry resources to help founders and executives stay competitive in a fast-changing market.
