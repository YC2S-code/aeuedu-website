Twenty years is long enough for a single seed to grow into a stretch of shade. AEU's story began in one classroom — and has been growing, quietly and steadily, ever since.

## Two Decades in the Making

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 2002 | The Asia Euro organization founded — one group of people, one starting point |
| 2003 | Recognized by Cambodia's Ministry of Education, upgraded to Asia Euro College |
| 2005 | Upgraded to Asia Euro University |
| Jan 2025 | AEU International Business School (AEUPS) established, reaching toward a wider horizon |
| 2026 | The inaugural EMBA cohort begins its journey alongside a new generation of peers |

Across two decades, AEU never rushed to grow large. Instead, it grew its roots deep — from a college, to a university, to today's regional international business school. Every step has been unhurried, but solid.

## Our Mission

We believe education isn't about handing out one more certificate. It's about walking alongside people who have built their businesses with their own hands, and helping them find their own direction.

The mission of AEU International Business School (AEUPS) is to help managers become leaders, operators become thinkers — and to connect businesses that once stood alone into a forest where everyone grows together.

## Our Commitment

We commit that every course is taught by people who genuinely know the field; that every study trip leads into markets that are real, not staged; and that every step of this shared journey is taken seriously.

We are not chasing scale. We are chasing the moment when everyone who walks through AEU's doors leaves with something gained — and new possibilities ahead.

## Our Philosophy: Educating Enterprises, Cultivating Talent

What does a business school actually teach? At AEU, the answer is this: education is never one-directional. It's a mutual achievement between enterprises and talent.

- Good enterprises need continuous learning to go further.
- Good talent needs to be tested in real business settings to truly grow.
- A good business school should be the bridge between the two.

This is the philosophy AEU has held to for twenty years. What we cultivate is never just "people who can manage" — it's entrepreneurs and leaders who understand business, hold a broad perspective, and know how to lead a team forward.

## Operating Entity

AME (雅凡·高级管理教育有限公司, Asia Management Education Co., Ltd.) exclusively operates and manages the EMBA Programme and the PEP (Professional Education Program) under AEU Postgraduate School.
