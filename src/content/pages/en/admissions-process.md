---
title: Admissions Process
---

Joining AEUEDU is a straightforward process designed to help families every step of the way.

## Step 1: Submit an Inquiry

Contact our admissions office or fill out the inquiry form on our [Contact page](/en/contact) to receive detailed information about our programs.

## Step 2: Campus Visit & Assessment

We invite prospective students and families for a campus tour, followed by a placement assessment to determine the right academic fit.

## Step 3: Application & Documents

Submit the completed application form along with required documents (academic records, identification, photos).

## Step 4: Offer & Enrollment

Successful applicants receive an offer letter. Enrollment is confirmed upon payment of the registration fee.
