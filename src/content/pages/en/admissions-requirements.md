---
title: Admissions Requirements
---

To apply for admission, please prepare the following documents:

- Completed application form
- Copy of birth certificate / ID card
- Recent academic transcripts or report cards
- Two recent passport-sized photos
- Health/vaccination record (for younger students)

## Age Requirements

Each program has specific age and grade-level requirements. Please contact our admissions office to confirm eligibility for your child's grade level.

## English/Chinese Proficiency

Depending on the program, an entry assessment may be required to determine the appropriate class placement.
