title: Tuition & Fees
---

We aim to provide transparent and competitive tuition pricing for all our programs.

## Fee Structure

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Registration Fee | One-time fee paid upon enrollment |
| Tuition Fee | Billed per semester or annually |
| Materials Fee | Covers textbooks and learning materials |
| Optional Services | Transportation, meals, extracurricular activities |

For detailed and up-to-date pricing information, please contact our admissions office directly via the [Contact page](/en/contact).

## Payment Methods

Tuition can be paid via bank transfer, in person at our finance office, or through our online payment portal (coming soon).
