*Last updated: June 2026*

Asia Euro University Postgraduate School (AEUPS), operating as AEU International Business School and managed by AME (Asia Management Education Co., Ltd.), is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard data when you visit our website or interact with our programs.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Identity information** — name, date of birth, nationality, passport or ID number
- **Contact information** — email address, phone number, mailing address
- **Academic & professional background** — education history, work experience, industry
- **Application data** — documents and materials submitted when applying to our programs
- **Website usage data** — browser type, IP address, pages visited, time on site (via cookies)
- **Communication records** — enquiries, messages, and correspondence sent to us

We collect information only to the extent necessary to provide our services.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

- Process and evaluate program applications
- Communicate program details, schedules, and updates
- Send newsletters, event invitations, or promotional materials (with your consent)
- Improve our website and services through analytics
- Comply with legal or regulatory obligations in Cambodia and relevant jurisdictions
- Verify identity and maintain institutional records

---

## 3. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and collect anonymous usage statistics. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function correctly.

We do not use cookies to collect personally identifiable information without your consent.

---

## 4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

- With service providers (e.g. event platforms, email systems) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements
- With academic partners or co-organising institutions, where you have applied for a joint program
- When required by Cambodian law, court order, or regulatory authority
- With your explicit prior consent

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

While we take security seriously, no transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to our website.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Application records are typically retained for a minimum of five (5) years.

---

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** — request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** — request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations
- **Withdraw consent** — opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us or clicking "Unsubscribe" in any email

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details below.

---
