### More Than Learning—A Chance to Grow as a Leader

Great leaders are not made by their job titles. They grow through making tough decisions, adapting to change, and handling challenges with confidence. As AI, global markets, and the business world continue to change, relying on past experience is no longer enough. To stay ahead, **today’s leaders need to keep learning, growing, and developing new ways of thinking.**

![](/images/uploads/3.png)

**The Entrepreneur Leadership Program (ELP)** at the Asia Euro University International Business School is designed for business owners and managers who want to grow as leaders. Through five core modules, you’ll strengthen your strategic thinking, business management, and leadership skills.

**ELP** is more than a course. It’s a place to develop better thinking, ask better questions, and make better decisions. Every module helps you gain new perspectives, and every class is an opportunity to grow as a leader.

- - -

### Pathway to EMBA

Complete all five ELP core modules and receive recognized transferable credits toward the Asia Euro University EMBAprogram with an official credit transfer letter.

### What You’ll Gain

1. **Strategic Thinking** – Spot opportunities and make smarter decisions.
2. **Leadership** – Lead people, teams, and change with confidence.
3. **Marketing** – Build stronger brands and customer value.
4. **Decision-Making** – Make better decisions with confidence.
5. **Public Speaking** – Communicate clearly and inspire others.

### **WHO IS THIS FOR**

* **Experienced Managers Ready for the Next Level**

  For professionals who have experience and good judgment but need a stronger leadership and business framework to drive future growth.
* **Next-Generation Business Leaders**

  For successors preparing to take over the family business or already stepping into leadership, building confidence and their own leadership style.
* **Decision-Makers Exploring an EMBA**

  A practical first step before an EMBA. Complete the ELP and seamlessly progress into the EMBA degree program.
* **Entrepreneurs Looking to Strengthen Their Leadership**

  For business owners who want to sharpen strategic thinking, expand their influence, and lead with greater confidence in a changing market.
