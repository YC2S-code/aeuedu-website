## Admissions: How to Apply

**Requirements**
- Bachelor's degree or above
- 8+ years of work experience
- 5+ years of management experience

**Application Process**

1. Initial inquiry and pre-registration
2. Submit application materials
3. Preliminary interview
4. Document review
5. Invitation to apply
6. Admissions interview
7. Results announced
8. Enrollment

Rolling admissions year-round. **Application deadline for the 2026 cohort: August 30, 2026.** Total tuition: USD 36,000.
