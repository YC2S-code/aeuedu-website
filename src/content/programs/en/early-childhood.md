Our Early Childhood Program creates a warm, stimulating environment where young learners develop foundational skills through play, storytelling, and guided exploration.

## Highlights

- Bilingual instruction (English & Chinese)
- Small class sizes with dedicated teaching assistants
- Focus on motor skills, language development, and social-emotional learning
